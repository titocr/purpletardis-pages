Media Center Backup

Privacy policy

Effective August 27, 2026.

Purpose and data handling

Media Center Backup is a private, owner-operated tool. It creates encrypted backups of self-hosted media-center application data and stores those encrypted backup objects in the owner’s Google Drive.

The tool uses Google Drive data only to create, read, verify, retain, and restore those backup objects. It does not sell, rent, advertise with, or share Google user data. It does not provide a public account system or access to media files.

Backup content is encrypted before upload. The owner controls the Google account and may remove the backup data or revoke this tool’s Google access at any time through Google Account settings.
